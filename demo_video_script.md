# Dust Demons — Demo Video Script
*Target: 2–3 minutes | Record on iPhone inside Jupiter Mobile*

---

## Scene 1: The Hook (0:00–0:15)
**Show:** Desktop browser → "MOBILE ONLY" gate screen

**Voiceover:**
> "Your Solana wallet is full of rugged tokens, dead airdrops, and useless dust. Every one of those token accounts is locking up SOL in rent. Dust Demons turns wallet cleanup into a game — and it's built exclusively for Jupiter Mobile."

---

## Scene 2: Open in Jupiter Mobile (0:15–0:30)
**Show:** Open `dust-demons.vercel.app` in Jupiter Mobile's in-app browser. Show the splash screen → onboarding tour briefly.

**Voiceover:**
> "We designed Dust Demons as a mobile-native experience. When you open it inside Jupiter Mobile, it instantly detects you're on Jupiter and activates a permanent 3x XP multiplier on everything you do."

---

## Scene 3: Connect Wallet (0:30–0:45)
**Show:** Tap "UPLINK MOBILE" → wallet connects instantly (no modal, no QR code). Show the wallet address appear + SOL balance.

**Voiceover:**
> "Authentication is seamless — one tap, and Jupiter Mobile's wallet adapter connects you instantly. No QR codes, no wallet selection modals. Pure frictionless Web3 UX."

---

## Scene 4: Scan & Identify Dust (0:45–1:15)
**Show:** Tap the scan button → watch the scanner animation → tokens appear color-coded (green = rent, yellow = dust, red = scam). Show Jupiter prices next to each token.

**Voiceover:**
> "The scanner uses the Helius DAS API to identify every token in your wallet, then prices them in real-time using Jupiter's Price API v3. Each token is categorized: green for rent-reclaimable, yellow for dust, red for scams."

---

## Scene 5: Burn Dust (1:15–1:45)
**Show:** Select some dust tokens → tap BURN → show the burn animation, loot drops (+XP notifications), confetti, and SOL reclaimed counter going up.

**Voiceover:**
> "Select your dust and hit burn. We close the token accounts on-chain, reclaiming the locked SOL rent directly to your wallet. Every burn earns XP, climbs you up the global leaderboard, and completes daily quests."

---

## Scene 6: Jupiter Swap → JupSOL Yield (1:45–2:10)
**Show:** Tap a tradeable token → swap view opens → Jupiter Terminal integrated → show the swap completing → "YIELD SECURED" modal.

**Voiceover:**
> "For tokens with real value, we integrate Jupiter's swap terminal directly in-app. Convert your dust into JupSOL and start earning 7% APY automatically. The swap mission completes and awards 500 XP."

---

## Scene 7: Prediction Market (2:10–2:30)
**Show:** Navigate to PROPHET view → show SOL price (from Jupiter API) → tap PUMP or DUMP → show the "WAGERED -50 XP" loot drop → timer counting down.

**Voiceover:**
> "We also built a prediction market powered by Jupiter's real-time price feeds. Players wager their earned XP on SOL price movements — win and earn 300 XP, lose and you're down 50. Real stakes, real engagement."

---

## Scene 8: Verify On-Chain (2:30–2:45)
**Show:** Open the menu → tap "VERIFY ON-CHAIN" → show the verification overlay → results: "✅ Jupiter Swaps, ✅ 3 Burns"

**Voiceover:**
> "Everything is verifiable. The 'Verify On-Chain' button reads your actual Solana transaction history to confirm burns and Jupiter swaps — no client-side faking. Your missions sync from the blockchain itself."

---

## Scene 9: Leaderboard & Close (2:45–3:00)
**Show:** Navigate to leaderboard → show global rankings → your rank highlighted → zoom out.

**Voiceover:**
> "Dust Demons — gamified wallet hygiene, built mobile-native on Jupiter. Clean your wallet, earn XP, climb the leaderboard. The Solana ecosystem is better when it's clean."

**End card:** DUST DEMONS logo + `dust-demons.vercel.app` + Jupiter logo

---

## Recording Tips
- **Record in Jupiter Mobile's in-app browser** (proves mobile-native)
- Use iPhone screen recording (Settings → Control Center → Screen Recording)
- Keep it under 3 minutes — judges watch dozens of these
- Show real tokens, real balances — don't fake it
- Background music: something cyberpunk/electronic, low volume
